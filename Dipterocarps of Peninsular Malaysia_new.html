Ecology

Canopy dominants

Dipterocarps form the emergent layer of lowland and hill dipterocarp forest — the signature ecosystem of Sundaland.

Timber

Meranti, Keruing, Balau

The family supplies most of Malaysia's commercial hardwood. Trade names group dozens of species by weight and working properties.

Conservation

Under pressure

Slow growth and high commercial value place most of the family on the IUCN Red List. Over half are Vulnerable or worse.

The nine* genera

*Fourteen genera reach Peninsular Malaysia. They are told apart mainly by the fruit — how many calyx lobes grow into wings, and how those wings sit on the nut.
