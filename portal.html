Trees of Malaysia

Two platforms. One shared database.

A botanical initiative by M. Nazre Saleh, Universiti Putra Malaysia. Explore the Dipterocarpaceae of Peninsular Malaysia through two complementary platforms — one open to the public, one a structured academic reference.

FREE · Open Access
Community Platform
Dipterokarp Malaysia
Open community database

A free, publicly accessible database of Malaysian Dipterocarps. Browse species, view field photographs contributed by the community, and explore IUCN conservation status and distribution records. Available in English and Bahasa Malaysia.

  • ✓Browse all 160+ species with photos
  • ✓IUCN status · state-level distribution
  • ✓Filter by genus, IUCN, endemism
  • ✓Contribute your own field photographs
  • ✓English & Bahasa Malaysia interface
  • ✗Full taxonomic descriptions
  • ✗Diagnostic keys & morphological data

Academic Reference
Trees of Peninsular Malaysia
Series I — Dipterocarpaceae

A structured interactive botanical reference for forestry students and researchers. Organised by Family → Genus → Species with full taxonomic descriptions, diagnostic characters, synonymy, and distribution — all following POWO 2026 and APG IV classification. Access requires a valid licence key.

  • ✓Full taxonomic & morphological descriptions
  • ✓Diagnostic characters & identification keys
  • ✓POWO 2026 accepted nomenclature
  • ✓Etymology of genus & species epithets
  • ✓Synonym history with publications
  • ✓Family → Genus → Species hierarchy
  • ✓Time-limited access — renew annually

Shared database: Both platforms draw from the same Supabase database. Field photographs approved in Dipterokarp Malaysia automatically appear in the interactive reference. Species data is updated in real time across both platforms.
